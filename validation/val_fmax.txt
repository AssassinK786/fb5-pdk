Reading: the OLD fit_hbt.py derived RB = fT/(8*pi*fMAX_target^2*(1.3*CJC)) and
printed fMAX_target as the result: fMAX was an INPUT, not a measurement. That
1.3x Cbc fudge forced RB=77 ohm, ~38% below IBM's measured Rb(100uA)=124 ohm,
so the old card really had fMAX 74.4 GHz instead of the claimed 60 GHz.
The fix: RB=124 ohm from measurement; fMAX is now EXTRACTED (63.9 GHz, IBM: 65).
CJS (collector-substrate) sits behind RC and adds an f^2 loss the simple
analytic fMAX=sqrt(fT/(8 pi RB Cbc)) formula ignores.
